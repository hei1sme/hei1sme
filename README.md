## Research

### SmokeNet · Published at AJCAI 2025

[Proactive Air Quality Forecasting and Health Alert System for Melbourne](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-981-95-4969-6_34) — Springer LNAI. **First & corresponding author.**

Gia-Hung Nguyen Le, Gia-Bao Pham Hoang, Tan-Phat Vo, Thu Le, Nhu Nguyen.

I designed the transformer model and chronological evaluation pipeline, then used attribution and counterfactual analysis to inspect its forecasts. The model uses 48 hours of history to forecast the next day's PM₂.₅.

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-981-95-4969-6_34) · [Research code](https://github.com/SpeedyLabX/melbourne-air-quality-forecast) · [Methods & evaluation](https://hei-portfolio.vercel.app/projects/smokenet-air-quality)
